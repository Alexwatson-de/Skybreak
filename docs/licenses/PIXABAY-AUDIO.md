The original recording was edited into six distinct game samples in `game/assets/audio/weapon_0.wav` through `weapon_5.wav`, with leading-silence removal, per-weapon pitch, equalization, filtering, envelopes and echo. They serve the pistol, assault rifle, shotgun, SMG, DMR and energy carbine respectively. The unedited source recording is not included. Jetpack, impact and explosion effects remain original synthesized sounds.
